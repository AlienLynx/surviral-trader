Allocator & market context ...

Policy grow (default): a bot that earns money (30+ trades, net positive now and over 7 days) is raised to 1.5x (2x with evidence edge); a bot that loses is stopped and gets a 0.5x trial when its control bot made money over the last 7 days (the control bot is control_long for a bot that trades long, control_short for one that trades short); the circuit breaker stops a bot that loses more than 1.25 average stakes, or 8 losing trades in a row, long before its 30-trade verdict; protection only scales (x0.5 below -15%, x0.25 below -25% from the high-water mark), it never idles the book. No LLM call. Policy protect: the original rules, an LLM proposes a weight 0..1 and hard rules (drawdown ladder, CPPI budget, macro window, opposite positions, ±25pp/day) have the last word. A run that finds a live bot uses protect automatically. Shadow = nothing is touched. Control bots and trend_ensemble are never managed.

LightGBM ranker paper only

The walk-forward study of this model (Binance perpetuals, hourly features, 3-day hold) passed every pre-registered check except a 54% drawdown. This is the forward test: every day it ranks the top-40 perpetuals, keeps two paper books (A plain, B volatility-scaled) and scores itself on data that did not exist when the model was designed. It never places an order. The verdict is computed by code; forward pass is a hypothesis for a longer test, never proof. The research behind it (v2 study, robustness grid across holds and universes, premium features) is on the Replay page, Studies tab.

Recent accounting decisions

Kronos feed live

Kronos-small forecasts all scored pairs every hour (container kronos-signal). The backend ranks them (24h relative strength, terciles) and publishes one file the bots read. Main, Shorts and Moonshot use it as their decision source (see each bot's page for the switch).

Announcement guard live

Exchange delisting notices are read every 10 minutes. A coin with a notice in force gets no new entry from Main, Shorts, Moonshot, Listing Fade and Trend v2; exits and open trades are untouched. A rule, not an LLM: the notice has a fixed form. If the feed breaks, nothing is blocked (fail-open) and the age below turns red.

Signal audit report only

Did a bot's buy / sell_short / hold on a pair beat the market over the next 1h / 4h / 24h? Forward returns use only candles after the decision; the benchmark is the mean return of all pairs the bot scanned at the same candle. Metrics appear at 200 decisions per direction; before that: "not enough data".
