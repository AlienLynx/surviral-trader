What this page is

Training and calibration of the research tools, separate from the tests on Replay. Everything here costs $0: no LLM is called. Everything is written only under /data/replay/; live bots, the ledger and the allocator are never touched.

  • Live reference: one read of the live bots' trades and decision logs, stored as a copy. The other steps work only on that copy.
  • Calibration: feeds the simulator the live bots' real decisions instead of an LLM and compares the trades 1:1, which separates simulator error from model error.
  • Kronos signal: historical Kronos forecasts and a pre-registered evaluation of whether they carry any edge.
The live reference and calibration make no claim about edge. The Kronos verdict is computed by code against thresholds fixed in advance and is shown as one badge: edge candidate unproven no edge insufficient n.

3 · Kronos as a measured signal (CPU only, no LLM, $0)

The container kronos-replay runs Kronos-small (24.7M, CPU, nice 10, idle without a job, weights baked into the image). For every hour of the window and every pair the model sees only candles before t0−1h. The signal is fixed in advance: |expected 4h return| ≥ 0.5% → up / down, otherwise neutral. The verdict is computed by code: net 4h excess over the mean of all pairs in the same hour, minus fees and 0.3% slippage, day-cluster bootstrap, n ≥ 200 per direction, CI lower bound > 0 and a positive result in both halves of the window.
