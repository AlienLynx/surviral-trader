What this page is, and what it does not prove

Replay is for tests only: an accelerated run of the LLM bots on historical candles, using the real strategy code (prompt, reply parsing, entry, rotation, stake) on data as it was at time t, with the bots' own exit constants. Training, the live reference, calibration and the Kronos signal work are on Training & calibration.

Nothing is traded and no live state changes: everything is written only under /data/replay/. There is no news for the past, so the prompt tells the model “unavailable for a historical run”. Replay never sets the status edge: the most it can say is unproven (replay).

Maker fills: can limit orders rescue the costs? (rules, $0, no LLM)

        
Costs decide every result here. A limit order pays a 0.02% maker fee and no slippage, but it is not always filled, and the orders that do not fill are the ones where the price ran away. Part 1 prices that for the LightGBM ranker book (limit 0.05 / 0.10 / 0.20% better than the 00:00 open, 1 / 2 / 4 hours to fill, then skip the trade or chase it as a taker; the primary is fixed in advance: 0.10%, 2 hours, chase). Verdict passive pays only if it is no worse than the optimistic taker book, survives skipped trades and at least half of the orders fill. Part 2 asks the same of the Listing Fade entries and shows whether the trades that would not fill are the better ones. Reads the caches of the LightGBM v2 study and of the Listing Fade study (no download); about 15 minutes.

Ranker robustness grid: is the LightGBM lead one lucky setting? (rules, $0, no LLM)

        
Re-runs the LightGBM v2 walk-forward for neighbouring settings (hold 2 / 3 / 5 / 7 days x universe 20 / 40 / 60 coins; the same features, fixed model and costs) and counts in how many the out-of-sample rank IC and the net APR stay positive. Verdict: neighbourhood holds (rank IC and APR positive in at least 10 of 12 cells), fragile (IC positive in fewer than 8), else mixed. Nothing is tuned; the 0.30% slippage column and the drawdown show whether the money survives. Reads the Binance cache of the v2 study (no download); about 7 minutes.

Premium-index features: do they add to the ranker? (rules, $0, no LLM)

        
The perpetual's premium to its index (Binance's free archive, hourly) is the market's own crowding gauge. One pre-registered comparison: the 39 v2 features against the same plus 8 premium features (hold 3 d, universe 40). Adopt only if the out-of-sample rank IC rises by at least 0.01, the APR at 0.30% slippage is not lower and the locked-period IC is not lower; otherwise the v2 features stay. Downloads about 15,000 small public files (no key, resumable, about 20 minutes), then about 5 minutes of CPU.

Listing Fade exit mechanics: tighter stop and re-entry cooldown (rules, $0, no LLM)

        
Live dry-run (4 weeks): 77% winners but break-even, because a few -12% stops (some gapping to -31%) cancel many small trailing wins, and the bot re-shorts a coin an hour after being stopped out.
          This study replays the live rules on the Listing Fade history (every Bybit listing since 2024, 5-minute candles, fees, funding, stop gaps, 3 slots, re-entry like the live bot) with 8 exit variants (stop -12 / -10 / -8 / -6% x no cooldown / 24 h cooldown after a stop-loss).
          Only one variant decides, fixed in advance: stop -8% + 24 h cooldown against the live rules, on listings from 2025-07-01 on (paired daily difference with a bootstrap CI, locked period not worse, drawdown not worse, still better at 0.50% slippage). Needs the Listing Fade data above (press Fetch data first). It changes nothing live.

The rule creates short candidates (the mirror of the long rule: change over 24 candles < −2 % and volatility < 5 %). The model can only confirm or veto; it cannot create a candidate or open a long. Exits are the parents'. The prompt has a funding line.
          Compared on one window: with the model's veto, rule only (no LLM, $0), random short entries. The question is whether the veto adds anything to the bare rule. Only candidates reach the LLM, so calls are few; the exact number is shown before the run. This is a replay: the bots are not started live (A3 only after you approve the report).
